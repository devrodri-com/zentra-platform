# Commercial decision boundary

Commercial rules and planning are intentionally not documented in this public repository.

```text
COMMERCIAL_DECISIONS_STATUS=OUTSIDE_PUBLIC_REPOSITORY
IMPLEMENTATION_COMMERCIAL_RULES=false
PRODUCTION_DATA_PRESENT=false
IDENTITY_AUTHORIZATION_FOUNDATION_BLOCKED_BY_COMMERCIAL_DECISIONS=false
```

Until an explicitly approved requirement is provided:

- do not implement product, account, order, subscription, fulfillment, promotion, tax, payment, or operational rules;
- do not invent values, content, fixtures, seeds, or test expectations;
- do not publish proposals, contracts, customer material, financial information, private decision records, or internal planning;
- use neutral technical scaffolding only.

Any future approved behavior must be documented at the minimum public level necessary for implementation without exposing private commercial material.

Products, prices, subscription terms, shipping and fulfillment rules, and
payment behavior remain open. They do not block the provider-agnostic identity,
account, membership, authorization, invitation, audit, or claim contracts in
ZP-03A. The foundation defines no commercial value or operational policy for
those future surfaces.
